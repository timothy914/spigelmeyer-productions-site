The Inner Thread

A cinematic documentary series by Spigelmeyer Productions.

The Inner Thread is a cinematic documentary series exploring how people across different cultures and historical periods have investigated the nature of the self, consciousness, knowledge, reality, and the human experience.

Through historical evidence, primary texts, philosophical inquiry, and contemplative traditions, the series examines ideas that have shaped humanity’s understanding of itself. It seeks connections between traditions without erasing their differences.
