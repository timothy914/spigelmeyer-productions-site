Steward of the Mirror

Spigelmeyer means steward of the mirror. The whole house is built on that idea: every book, garment, and design is made to reflect something true, not just to look good.

Spigelmeyer Productions is a veteran-owned creative productions company, founded and run by U.S. Navy veteran Timothy Lee Spigelmeyer Jr.
